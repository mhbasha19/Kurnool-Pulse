Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Kurnool Pulse ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a personal data analytics project. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our use of the Google Ads API.

Information We Access

  • We access publicly available, aggregated keyword search volume data through the Google Ads API.
  • We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from end users.
  • We use Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication to access API services.

Use of Google Ads API Data

  • Data retrieved via the Google Ads API is used solely for internal analytics and research purposes.
  • We do not sell, share, or distribute any data obtained through the API.
  • We comply with the Google Ads API Terms of Service and all applicable Google API policies.

Data Storage

  • Aggregated, non-personal search trend data is stored locally in a SQLite database for analysis purposes.
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or stored.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Ads API: Subject to Google's Privacy Policy

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date.
